**New since v1.14.0: Mystery Curves — ready-made rosettes of 2- to 7-fold symmetry in the Composer.** The Composer's **Stack ▾** menu now offers Frank Farris's "mystery curve" from *Creating Symmetry* and its relatives: one click turns the figure into a rosette that repeats itself exactly 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7 times around its centre. Sparks now show again under Neon Lines and Ribbon when the figure is hidden, the period readout under each wave follows the Speed slider, and three new presets show the rosettes.

## Mystery Curves

- The entries under **Mystery Curve (Farris) — a new figure** at the bottom of **Stack ▾** replace the whole figure
  with a rosette: **2-, 3-, 4-, 5-, 6- or 7-fold**. The 5-fold one is Farris's own curve.
- Each of the three waves becomes one turning circle; their speeds differ by multiples of the fold, which is what makes
  the figure come round to the same shape every 1/fold of a turn. Particle Number moves to a multiple of the fold so the
  symmetry is exact.
- The figure you had is kept in History, and render mode, material and look are left as they were.
- The manual's new section *Rosettes — the Mystery Curve* explains how it works and which settings keep the symmetry
  (Size, Shift, Pulse on a wave's X and Y alike) and which break it (Wobble, a changed winding).

## Fixes

- **Sparks under Neon Lines and Ribbon:** a fire material's sparks were drawn black there, so they vanished — plainly
  with the figure hidden (Dye Field → Show Figure off), unnoticed in the figure's own glow with it shown. They now
  draw in their colours and at their own size.
- **The period readout follows Speed:** *repeats every … s* and *slowest cycle … s* under each wave are now the time you
  actually watch, Speed included. The seconds on the Pulse and Wobble rows still set the figure's own timing.
- **The Mixer at the ends of the crossfader:** every blended number now arrives exactly at deck A or deck B at the two
  ends, instead of a hair away.
- **Running the app from source:** building the screensaver no longer stops a running development server.

## New presets

- **Mystery Curves:** Mystery Curve 3, Mystery Curve 5, Mystery Curve 7.
