**New since v1.3.0: 3-D Symmetry, an editor for your own self-similar curves, and Electric Discharge.** Repeat any figure in three dimensions on a ring, a sphere or a solid; design a curve generator step by step and watch it grow; and tear Neon Lines into crackling filaments. Twenty-three new demo presets show them off.

## 3-D Symmetry

The figure can now be **repeated in three dimensions around the centre** — *Visual FX → Deformation*,
directly below the 2-D Symmetry. You choose two things separately:

- **Where the copies sit:** on a **Ring** of 1 to 24 positions, on a **Sphere** of 2 to 60, or on the
  points of a solid — **Tetrahedron**, **Octahedron**, **Cube**, **Icosahedron** or **Dodecahedron** —
  using its vertices, the middles of its edges or the centres of its faces.
- **How many copies share each position:** 1 to 12, turned evenly about it, with **3-D Mirrors** to make
  every second one a mirror image.

**3-D Radius** moves the copies apart (at 0 they overlap into one symmetric solid) and **Facing** turns
each copy outward or keeps them parallel. Each copy is the complete figure, never cut, and the whole
arrangement turns with it. The line under the rows multiplies it out — *6 positions × 3 = 18 copies* — and
says how many particles each copy gets.

## Your own self-similar curves: Customize

The curve figure type gains **Customize**, an editor for the curve's **generator** — the short sequence of
moves that is repeated at every level. Pick it under *Rule* in the Composer, start from the current rule
and change it: add a move or a retrace, change a step's turn, length or direction, give the curve a
**starting shape**, and watch the result update live. A status line says when a generator cannot work and
why, and a density readout says how evenly the curve covers space. Customize can start from every rule
except Bush 3D, the one growth rule.

Also new on the curve figure type:

- **Twist** turns chosen copies of the curve about their own axis at every level at once — on the Dragon,
  a half-turn *is* the Lévy C curve, so one slider folds one named fractal into another.
- **Stretch** lengthens or shortens chosen copies, so a curve breathes in and out.
- **Two more rules:** **Koch** and **Minkowski**, for fifteen in all.

## Electric Discharge

A new effect in *Visual FX*, directly below **Neon Lines**: it tears the lines' glow into **crackling,
flickering filaments**, from a faint shimmer to forking, lightning-like arcs. Four controls — *Intensity*,
*Filament*, *Crackle* and *Branching*. It works on Neon Lines, so switching it on switches the lines on too.

## Smaller changes in this release

- **Neon Lines are drawn on the graphics card**, so line-drawn figures with many particles run at full
  speed.
- **Depth cues** (Depth Sz, Depth Br, DoF, Color Sh) apply to particles and splats; on a figure drawn with
  Neon Lines or Ribbons they have no effect.
