**New since v1.13.0: Arrange copies — one figure, many copies along a path.** A new panel on the Composer's Detail wave turns any figure you draw into 2 to 60 copies placed along a circle, a figure eight, a pretzel, a saddle ring, a real knot or a path of your own, and can let the copies change shape along the way. Particle Number now has a field for typing an exact count, a tuned Particle Number or Rotation now survives Export, and eight new presets show the copies and more.

## Arrange copies

- **Arrange copies…** on the Composer's **Detail** slot uses the Detail wave as a layout: the figure Body and
  Structure draw is repeated along a path, every copy drawn with the same number of particles.
- **Layout:** Ellipse / Circle (with an **Aspect**), Figure Eight, Pretzel, Saddle Ring (a circle that rises and falls
  twice), Lissajous Knot (a real knot that never touches itself) or Custom (three whole numbers, one per axis).
- **Copies** 2–60 and **Spread** (the size of the path). Particle Number moves to the nearest exact multiple of the copy
  count by itself.
- **Morph** X, Y or Z lets the copies change shape gradually around the path, through 1–3 full cycles.
- After the first **Arrange** every control applies at once. **Remove copies** takes the arrangement out again; both
  leave a History snapshot, so either is one click to undo.
- A **status line** reads the figure itself, so it stays right whatever changed it — it names the arrangement, says
  when a hand-typed Particle Number has joined the copies up, and warns when the copies overlap.
- The **Shape** diagram draws an arrangement as its separate copies.
- The section *Many copies from one figure — Arrange copies* in the user manual explains how the effect works, and
  which other settings keep the copies apart or join them up.

## Particle Number and Rotation

- **An exact Particle Number field** sits beside the slider on Visual FX and on the Composer: type any whole number
  from 1 to 50 000 and press Enter. The slider still moves in steps of 100.
- **Fixed:** a Particle Number or Rotation changed in the Lab was lost when the preset was exported or snapshotted —
  the export kept the preset's earlier value. Both are now saved.

## New presets

- **Arranged copies:** Copies Figure Eight, Copies Knot, Copies Saddle, Copies Morph Ring.
- **Copies built by hand:** Candystore, Lord of the Rings.
- **Spherical spirals:** Earth at Night, Dancing Lovers.
