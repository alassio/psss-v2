## New: self-similar curves, a second figure type

A preset can now draw a **self-similar curve**, built by repeating a production rule, instead of the
sum of sine waves. Pick the figure type at the top of the Composer.

- **Thirteen rules:** Hilbert 2D and 3D, the dragon, the terdragon, the quadratic Koch curve, the
  Sierpiński arrowhead, Gosper, a branching plant, Moore, Peano, Lévy C, Sierpiński–Knopp and Bush 3D.
- **Animated by** three independent turning angles, a continuous recursion depth and a self-similar
  zoom (*Descend*).
- **The angle is not part of the rule**, so sweeping it morphs one named fractal into another: the Koch
  production at 90° is the Koch curve, and at 117° it is a Sierpiński triangle.
- **Everything else applies unchanged:** deformation, the field systems, every colour source and
  render mode, trails, the image warp and the grade.
- **Switching type loses nothing.** The other type's settings are kept, so switching back restores the
  figure exactly.
- **Nine demo presets** at the end of the library (334 presets in all): *Curve Lattice*, *Curve Descent*,
  *Curve Gasket*, *Curve Island*, *Curve Thicket*, *Curve Volume*, *Curve Tilt*, *Curve Subdivide* and
  *Curve Morph*.
