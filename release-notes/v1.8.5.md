**Faster liquid presets, a preview grid that no longer animates thumbnails you cannot see, and a fix for presets with a Translation Map force in full screen.** The heaviest liquid presets draw a quarter of the pixels they did; the preview grid gives way to the main view sooner and stops animating off-screen thumbnails; and the performance readout now shows the brief freezes it used to hide.

## Faster liquid presets

A liquid preset builds its surface from every particle drawn as a soft disc. On the presets with large discs —
**Boiling Milk**, **Hypno Penta**, **Flower Power II**, **Cycloid II** and a few others — that meant hundreds of
millions of pixels per frame. Those discs are now drawn at half resolution, which is a quarter of the pixels, while
the surface itself is still shaded at full resolution. Liquids with small discs, such as **Liquid Maze** and
**Growing Corals**, are unchanged. Compared on exactly the same frame, the pictures are practically identical; the
largest differences are faint ones along the rim highlights of **First Contact** and **Fluid**.

## The preview grid

- **Thumbnails you cannot see are no longer animated.** When the grid jumped to the current preset — at start-up,
  after a search, after a reorder — thumbnails it scrolled past could keep animating out of sight for the rest of
  the session, especially with display scaling at 125–150 %. Now only the thumbnails on screen, plus one row ready
  for scrolling, are animated.
- **The main view comes first sooner.** The thumbnails now give way as soon as the main view starts missing frames,
  and stop animating before it stutters; they still all show their picture. On a 120 or 144 Hz screen, a main view
  holding 60 fps counts as on time, so thumbnails keep animating on fast machines.

## Fixed

- **Presets with a Translation Map force behaved wrongly after a resize, including going full screen.** The force
  was still computed for the old window size, so it pushed the wrong part of the figure. This affected nine presets,
  among them **Rising Inferno**, **Lava Flow**, **Firestorm** and **Smoke Cathedral**.

## The performance readout (`?perf=1`)

- **`worst 10 s`** on the first line: the longest single frame of the last ten seconds. It shows brief freezes, such
  as the moment a reel switches to the next preset, which the other two figures cannot.
- Frame times are now the real ones. A frame longer than 100 ms used to be shown as 100 ms.
- Two more rows: **`trail`** (fading the previous frames) and **`present`** (on presets without a grade).
