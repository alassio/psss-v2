Find any setting from the Synthesis Lab's header, take one part of a preset into another in one click in the Mixer, and five more self-similar-curve demos.

## New

- **Find a setting.** A search field in the Synthesis Lab's header finds any control across every tab
  by its name, its tooltip or a value it offers. It shows where the control lives and jumps there,
  opening closed groups on the way. Rows that can't be reached in the current state say why.
- **Mixer recipes.** *Figure from B, look from A* and the reverse, as one click each, plus *Reset* back
  to where you started. Every category row has an **A / ⇄ / B** selector, and a sentence says where each
  part of the preview currently comes from.
- **Consistent category names** in the Mixer, Randomise and Evolution.
- **Five more self-similar-curve demos:** *Curve Weave*, *Triad*, *Dragonfold*, *Scroll* and
  *Plane Dive* (339 presets in all).

## Fixed

- **Depth Brightness** and **Depth Colour Shift** pointed the wrong way (near was dimmer and cooler),
  which cancelled the size cue. All depth cues now agree.
- **Lévy C, Sierpiński–Knopp and the dragon** stopped at recursion 8, whatever the *Recursion* row said.
- **The Composer** could show wave controls under a curve preset.
- **A locked Figure category in the Mixer** now holds a self-similar curve too, not only the waves.
- **After a Mixer reset** the Deck B dropdown read empty while Deck B was still in use.
