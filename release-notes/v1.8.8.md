**No more periodic stalls on the presets that read data back from the graphics card.** Presets with a Translation Map force (such as **Lava Flow**), with fluid advection (such as **The Real Super Man**) or with physarum (such as **Physarum Flower**) no longer pause every few frames while the graphics card catches up; and the performance test log now reports the screen's refresh rate correctly.

## Fixed

- **Periodic stalls on 11 presets.** Three effects read data back from the graphics card: the **Translation Map
  force** driven by a flow field (**Lava Flow**, **Rising Inferno**, **Firestorm**, **Smoke Cathedral**, **Imploding
  Sun**, **Age Gate Embers**), **fluid
  advection** (**The Real Super Man**, **Hypno Penta II**, **Advection Carry**) and **physarum** (**Physarum Flower**,
  **Physarum Weave**). Each read made the whole frame wait until the graphics card had finished all its work. On the
  test machines **Lava Flow** ran smoothly most of the time but stalled for 67–200 ms every few frames. The reads
  now happen in the background: the frame continues, and the data is picked up a frame or two later. The effects
  look the same; because they react to slightly later data, the swirls take slightly different paths.
- **The performance test log (`?reel=2`)** reported a wrong refresh rate (294 Hz on a 120 Hz screen). It now
  reads it from the frames themselves.
