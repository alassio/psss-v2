**New since v1.11.0: plants that grow, two tetrahedron curves, plants that grow outward under 3-D Symmetry, and Customize for every plant.** Eight plants from *The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants* join the curves, and any curve can now grow on screen — a plant generation by generation from its trunk, the others unfolding in place. The Sierpinski and Hill tetrahedra are new curves. 3-D Symmetry can hold each copy by its root, so plants burst outward from one point. And Customize now edits a plant's own rules as rows of symbols. 22 new presets show it all.

## Plants and growth

- **Eight plants from the book**, each checked against its published definition: **Sprig**, **Thicket Weed**,
  **Fork Tree**, **Fir Shoot**, **Wind Weed**, **Wild Plant** (a random plant: every **Seed** grows a different
  specimen), **Flowering Plant** (leaves and five-petal flowers, drawn as outlines) and the corrected **Bush 3D**.
  The earlier reading of the bush stays, renamed **Coral Bush**, so presets built on it look exactly as before.
- **Growth** (Composer → Curve) makes any curve grow on screen: a plant generation by generation — the trunk first,
  then its branches, then theirs, leaves and flowers last — and every other curve unfolding in place, each segment
  folding into the next level while the ends stay fixed. **Grow once**, **Loop** or **Loop, new plant each time**, with a
  **Growth time** and a **Pause**; on a loop the plant withers back tips-first. A preset starts growing again every
  time it is shown, so the reel and the screensaver show each one grow; the preview grid shows them fully grown.
- **Taper** makes the twigs finer than the trunk, and **Seed** picks which specimen a random plant grows.

## Two tetrahedron curves

- **Sierpinski Tetrahedron** — a lacy shell of nested pyramids; seen straight down one of its axes it reads as a
  square lattice.
- **Hill Tetrahedron** — one continuous line that completely fills a tetrahedron that is one sixth of a cube.

## 3-D Symmetry: Grow from

A new **Grow from** row chooses where each copy is held. **Figure centre** is the familiar arrangement. **Curve
start** puts every copy's root on the centre and points its stem outward, so a plant's copies grow out of one point
instead of in through each other; with a **3-D Radius** they sprout from the points of the solid. It applies to the
self-similar curves; on other figures the row is greyed out.

## Customize for every plant

**Customize** now works on every plant. It opens a **production editor**: the plant's own rules as rows of coloured
symbols — the **Axiom** it starts from, then one row per letter saying what it becomes at each level. Click between
symbols and add from the palette or type; brackets and leaf braces always come in pairs. **+ Alternative** makes a
row random, with a weight for each alternative. An edit that would leave the plant drawing nothing is refused, with
the reason shown. **Plant**, **Sprig** and **Thicket Weed** can also be edited as a generator (**Edit as**), and an
edited plant generator still grows and tapers like the plant.
