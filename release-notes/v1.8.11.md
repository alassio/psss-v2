**Faster fire, smoke and glow, and the Julia and Mandelbrot flow presets no longer get stuck at half speed.** The smoke and flame turbulence costs less on every fire preset, the glow is one full-screen pass cheaper on almost every preset, and the escape-time flow presets reach their full frame rate reliably. Nothing looks different at normal frame rates.

## Faster

- **Fire and smoke.** The turbulence that moves flames and smoke is computed with faster lookups, giving exactly the
  same result. On the test machine **Curtains Falling** went from 70 to 90 fps. All 23 fire and smoke presets
  benefit, most of all the large ones such as **Ghosts**, **Flame**, **Dream Flash** and **Quader Star Twelve**.
- **Glow.** The glow is now added while the colours are graded, instead of in a separate full-screen pass. The
  graphics time for glow and grade fell by 25–30 % on every preset that uses them; the gain is largest on
  integrated graphics.
- **Round splats** (the Ice materials and a few others) are drawn as octagons, so the invisible corners are no longer
  computed.
- **Liquid cohesion** (the beading of liquid presets) is about 12 % cheaper.

## Fixed

- **Julia and Mandelbrot flow presets could get stuck at half speed.** Their flow took more steps per frame whenever
  a frame was slow, which made the next frame slow too, so after a single slow moment (such as switching to the preset)
  they could stay at half the frame rate. On the test machine **Mandelbrot River Glow** ran at 26 fps in one test and
  60 fps in another, with nothing changed. The flow now takes at most one step per frame. At 60 fps and above nothing
  changes; on a machine that cannot reach 60 fps, the contours drift more slowly instead of the frame rate dropping.
