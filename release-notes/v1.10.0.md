**New since v1.8.11: attractors drawn as one flowing strand, four new closed self-similar curves, and attractors in the preview tiles.** A Lorenz, Rössler, Thomas, Halvorsen, Aizawa or Nosé-Hoover attractor can now be drawn as a single stroke that travels along its own path, with its colour running along it; the Koch snowflake, the pentigree, the twindragon and the fudgeflake join the curve library; and the preview grid now shows every attractor preset as its attractor rather than as its wave figure. Twelve new demo presets show them off.

## Attractors as a strand

*Visual FX → Figure → Attractor* has a new **Layout** row:

- **Cloud** is the familiar look: the particles are spread over many unrelated orbits of the attractor.
- **Strand** follows **one** trajectory, so neighbouring particles are neighbouring points along the path. The
  attractor becomes a single moving stroke with two free ends, and a palette mapped by **particle index** runs
  as a gradient along it.

**Strand Length** sets how much of the trajectory is shown and **Strand Speed** how fast the strand travels
(0 freezes it); both can breathe over time. A strand works on the six flowing attractors; the Clifford, De Jong,
Julia and Mandelbrot presets keep drawing a cloud, and the row says so.

Because the wave figure and the strand are both ordered strokes, a mid **Blend** now gives one stroke between
the two — drag Blend from 0 to 1 to morph a wave figure into the attractor point by point.

New demos: **Lorenz Strand**, **Nosé-Hoover Fan**, **Halvorsen Thread**, **Aizawa Overwound** and
**Lorenz Strand Morph**.

## Four new closed curves

The self-similar curve type gains four rules that close into a loop: the **Koch snowflake** (the Koch curve
round a triangle), the **pentigree** (the library's first five-fold figure), the **twindragon** (two dragons
back to back) and the **fudgeflake** (three terdragons round a triangle). All four can be opened in
**Customize**, which shows their pattern on its starting shape.

New demos: **Curve Snowflake**, **Curve Pentigree**, **Curve Twindragon**, **Curve Fudgeflake**, and three
Customize curves — **Curve Golden Dragon**, **Curve Golden Lightning** and **Curve Zigzag Cross**.

## Improved

- **Attractors in the preview tiles.** Attractor presets used to show their wave figure in the grid, which could
  look nothing like the preset. Tiles now draw a lighter version of the attractor — cloud or strand — so the grid
  matches what you will see.
- **Three duplicate presets removed.** *Black Hole Contours*, *Mandelbrot River Contours* and *Siegel Disk
  Contours* had become exact copies of *Birth of a Black Hole*, *Mandelbrot River* and *Julia Siegel Disk River*.
