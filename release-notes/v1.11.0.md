**New since v1.10.0: choose the Mixer's decks from live preview tiles, two new presets, and clearer Transmap Strength feedback.** Each Mixer deck now opens a picker with a search field, the preview grid's filter and live tiles in the grid's current order. A Deck A choice now shows in the main view straight away. *Jagged Glass* and *Skin of a Snake* join the library.

## Mixer: choose a deck from live tiles

Click **Deck A** or **Deck B** and a picker opens over the Mixer:

- every preset as a live preview tile, in the **same order as the preview grid** (its current **Sort**);
- a **search field** that narrows the tiles by name;
- the preview grid's **Filter** chips. A filter set here also filters the grid, and the other way round.

Click a tile to load it into the deck; **Close** or **Esc** leaves without changing anything.

## New presets

- **Jagged Glass**, developed from *Rippled Rays Of Light 3*.
- **Skin of a Snake**, developed from *Chain Warp Refract*.

## Improved

- **Transmap Strength.** On a layer that uses the preset's own shift table, the tab now says how strongly the
  rows act at the current Strength. For example: *"Strength 0.75: the shift amplitudes below act ×1.50 — the
  rows show them as authored."*

## Fixed

- **Choosing Deck A changed nothing on screen until a Deck B was chosen.** A new Deck A now shows in the main
  view straight away.
